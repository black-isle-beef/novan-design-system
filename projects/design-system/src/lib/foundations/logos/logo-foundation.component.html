Usage Guidelines

Do

  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least its own width.
  • Use the primary lockup at 120px wide or larger; below that, use the mark alone.
  • Choose the mark color from the Color & Background Pairing section above.
  • Swap to the compact lockup at tablet and mobile widths.

Don't

  • Don't recolor the mark's purple accent triangle.
  • Don't stretch, skew, or rotate the mark or wordmark.
  • Don't place a low-contrast variant on a similarly-toned background.
  • Don't rebuild the wordmark in a different typeface — use Montserrat.
