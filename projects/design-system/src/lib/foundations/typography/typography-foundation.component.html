Body & Inline Text

Lead paragraphs use a slightly larger, lighter Montserrat weight to introduce a section.

Body copy is set in Regular 400 for maximum readability across long-form content. It supports bold/strong emphasis, italic/emphasis, underline, strikethrough, and a highlighter for callouts.

Small print — captions, timestamps, and legal copy.
